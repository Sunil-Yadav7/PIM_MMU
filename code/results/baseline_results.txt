Key agreements with the paper:
  - Full PIM-MMU: up to 4.5x (D->P) / 6.7x (P->D); paper avg 3.3x/3.8x.
  - A plain DCE (+D) is SLOWER than Base (paper's key ablation result).
  - HetMap alone (+D+H) does not help; PIM-MS unlocks the gain.
  - Baseline bottoms out at 8.3 GB/s (14% of peak) at 256 MB vs paper 8.9/15.5%.
  - Software baseline is contention-fragile; PIM-MMU is flat.
